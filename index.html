From concept to completion, every step of the process is built for ease, precision, and customization. Our 3D customizer puts the creative process in your hands—allowing you to bring your vision to life effortlessly. Start designing today and craft a hat that represents your brand with style and distinction.

Design Your Hat Basics

Create the foundation of your design by selecting the core elements of your hat—from the crown color to the rope style and backstrap. These features define the base look and feel of your custom piece.
1

Choose a Crown Color

The foundation of your hat starts with the crown color. Whether you're after a refined neutral, a bold statement, or something in between, our carefully curated selection of shades ensures you’ll find the perfect match.

2

Choose a Rope Style

A rope detail can add a touch of personality. Opt for a clean, rope-free look, or select from a variety of styles and colors to create subtle contrast or a bold accent.

3

Choose a Backstrap Color

More than functional, the backstrap adds style. Match your crown or choose a bold contrast—it keeps your hat cohesive from every angle.

Customize Your Patch

Now it’s time to tailor your patch. Choose a material, define its shape, and fine-tune the colors that will frame your logo. These elements ensure your branding is clear, distinctive, and cohesive.
4

Select a Patch Type & Shape

The patch is the focal point of your design. Choose between PVC or Fabric, each offering a distinct texture and aesthetic. Then, select a round, square, or rectangular shape to best complement your logo.

5

Select an Outline
Patch Color

The outline frames your patch, enhancing its definition and contrast. Whether you prefer a subtle border that blends in or a bold outline that makes a statement, this step ensures the patch stands out in the best way.

Your Brand, Your Hat

Your logo is what makes your hat truly yours. Select a background color that enhances its visibility, then upload your logo to preview the final look before you commit.
6

Choose an Inline Patch Color

The inline color serves as the backdrop for your logo, influencing its visibility and impact. Select a shade that complements your branding while keeping your design polished and balanced.

7

Upload Your Logo

This is where your design comes to life. Upload your brand’s logo and get a real-time preview of how it will look on the finished product.

Finalize & Personalize

Once your design is ready, select your quantity and place your order. Whether you’re ordering 25 or scaling up to 100+, the process remains smooth, simple, and transparent.
8

Select a Quantity & Place Your Order

Our minimum order is 25 hats, making it easy to start. Ordering 50 or more? Enjoy exclusive volume pricing. The volume discounts will be displayed on the cart and checkout page. Once your design is finalized, confirm your selection and proceed to checkout.

Confirm & Produce

We review every detail before production begins. Once confirmed, your order moves into production and payment is handled securely through QuickBooks—with flexible terms for your convenience.
9

Order Confirmation

Once your order is placed, our team will get in touch with you to confirm the details before production begins, ensuring everything is exactly as you envisioned.

10

Payment & Production

To keep things simple, we offer flexible 30-day payment terms via QuickBooks. Once payment is processed, your custom hats go into production, crafted with precision and care.
